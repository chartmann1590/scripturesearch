ScriptureSearch Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Overview

ScriptureSearch ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

ScriptureSearch is designed with privacy in mind. The app operates primarily offline and minimizes data collection:

  • Local Data Only: Your Bible reading history, bookmarks, notes, and search history are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or sync this data to any server.
  • No Account Required: ScriptureSearch does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use the app.
  • No Cloud Storage: None of your personal data, reading preferences, or app usage is stored in the cloud.

Data Usage

Since ScriptureSearch operates offline-first:

  • We do not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.
  • Your Bible translations and study materials are downloaded to your device for offline access.
  • Any data you enter into the app (notes, highlights, bookmarks) remains on your device only.

Third-Party Services

ScriptureSearch may use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services: For app distribution and updates.
  • Firebase (optional): If enabled, Firebase may collect anonymous analytics data. You can disable analytics in the app settings.
  • Text-to-Speech Services: If you use the audio Bible feature, your device's built-in text-to-speech engine may process text locally.

Permissions

ScriptureSearch may request the following permissions:

  • Storage/Media: To download Bible translations for offline reading.
  • Internet (optional): Only if you choose to enable cloud sync or download additional content.

All permissions are optional and can be denied without affecting core app functionality.

Children's Privacy

ScriptureSearch is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app contains no advertising directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you can delete all your data by:

  • Uninstalling the app from your device
  • Clearing the app's data in your device settings

No data is retained on our servers after app uninstallation.
